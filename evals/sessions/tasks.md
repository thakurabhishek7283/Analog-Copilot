# Your tasks

You are testing an electronics tutor, not being tested. Think aloud: say what you are looking for and
what surprises you. Ask the tutor anything, in your own words, as often as you like. After each answer,
click **Yes** or **No** under it: was it helpful?

**1. Build a filter (8 min).**
Insert a *Sallen-Key low-pass* block with a 1 kHz cutoff, and drive it with a *Sine signal source*
block connected to its input. When both checks show ✓, select one resistor and ask the tutor why it
has the value it has. Ask a follow-up question if the answer leaves you unsure.

**2. Let the tutor guide you (5 min).**
Switch the Ask panel to **Guide me**. Ask why the output is smaller than the input at 1 kHz. Answer the
tutor's question in your next message, until you can explain it in your own words.

**3. Change the cutoff (7 min).**
Ask the tutor how to move the cutoff to 500 Hz. If it suggests an experiment, read its prediction,
then click **Try it**. Did the measured result match the prediction? Undo it afterwards if you like.

**4. Your own change (5 min).**
Change any part value you are curious about. When the simulation has run, click **What changed?** and
read the explanation. Was it what you expected?

**5. Something broke (5 min).**
Look away for a moment while the facilitator changes something. Then find out what is wrong with the
circuit and fix it, using the tutor however you like.

**Time left?** Insert any other block and ask about it, or try **Deep** thinking on a hard question.
